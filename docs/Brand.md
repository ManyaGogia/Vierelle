
# Vierelle Brand Guide

## Mission

Elevate everyday beauty into meaningful rituals.

## Vision

To become the most elegant destination for modern beauty rituals.

## Brand Personality

- Calm
- Elegant
- Luxurious
- Minimal
- Honest
- Intentional

## Brand Voice

We speak softly.

We educate instead of selling.

We inspire instead of convincing.

We create rituals, not routines.

## Never

- Loud
- Pushy
- Trendy slang
- Fake urgency
- Excessive discounts
